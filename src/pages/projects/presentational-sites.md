### What it is

Over the years, several smaller clients needed straightforward presentational ("brochure") sites: a public-facing site to showcase their business, services, and contact details, without the complexity of a web app. This covers a handful of those builds: **HecasVillas.ro**, **Offroadmania.ro**, **Aled.ro**, and **Melissia.ro**.

### Tech stack

- **WordPress** as the CMS for each site
- Custom themes and page layouts tailored to each client's branding and content

### What it set out to achieve

- Take each client's specification (branding, pages, content) and turn it into a working site
- Keep each site easy for the client to maintain afterwards
- Deliver a range of distinct designs across different industries (villas/accommodation, off-road tourism, and others) from a shared WordPress foundation
